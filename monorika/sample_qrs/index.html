Apple App Review Test Sheet

Monorika - Sample Test QR Codes

Monorika is a local-first storage management application that lets users inspect physical storage box contents and identify items through camera QR scanning without opening boxes.

How to Test with Your iOS Device:
  1. Launch Monorika on your test iPhone. The app starts directly in the QR Scanner (QRスキャン) view.
  2. Point your iPhone camera at one of the three QR codes below.
  3. Scan Storage Box #01 to see its three seeded items, or scan Individual Item #01 to see its home location.
  4. Scan Unregistered QR to open the new-code registration sheet and start registering an item or a storage box.
  5. From Settings (設定), you can also preview A4 QR labels and test backup/restore.
📦 1. Storage Box Quick Check
Scan the code to inspect the box contents without opening the physical container.
STORAGE BOX #01
Winter Tops (クローゼット中段)
Bedroom closet / 3 seeded clothing items
Expected Result: The bottom sheet shows three stored items: a cashmere turtleneck, a mohair sweater, and a wool cardigan.
🏷️ 2. Identify an Item and Find Its Home
Scan an individual item's QR code to see where it belongs.
INDIVIDUAL ITEM #01
Software Design Handbook (実践ソフトウェア設計ハンドブック)
Stored in the Important Binder (書類・重要バインダー)
Expected Result: The item sheet shows the book's designated storage location. The reviewer can try the take-out and return actions.
⚡ 3. Register a New QR Sticker
Scan an unused code to try the first-time registration flow.
UNREGISTERED QR
New Sticker (未登録QR)
This code is not present in the seeded demo data.
Expected Result: The app opens the "新しいQRコードを検知" sheet. The reviewer can start registering the code as an item or a storage box.
